Two ideas for the AI animation

A · Zoom: from the data centre to one multiplication

The camera follows one request down six levels, one on screen at a time, then the answer streams back up. This still is the last level: the AMX unit inside one Xeon core, multiplying a tile of the prompt by a tile of the model's weights.

Moving version: about 3 seconds per level, the request glows as it goes down; then the answer's words rise back up through the levels. Pause and step buttons as on the migration drawing.

B · Serving timeline: three users share one server

Time runs left to right. Three requests arrive, wait, process their prompt and stream their answers, sharing one loaded model. When one finishes, the next one starts. Below, the server's shared resources fill and empty as it happens.

Moving version: the "now" line sweeps right, bars grow behind it, tokens tick out of each lane into short answer text on the right. An optional "Inside the computation" button opens a close-up of one matrix multiply.
