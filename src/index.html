Research

Publications, patents, education

A decade modelling what migration actually costs

Doctorate in IT Systems Engineering at the Hasso-Plattner Institute under Professor Christoph Meinel. The thesis uses machine learning to model and predict the overhead of live migration: moving a running virtual machine from one server to another without switching it off.

It began as an academic question about overhead. It describes the same capacity problem enterprises meet when they move workloads, AI included, onto shared infrastructure.
