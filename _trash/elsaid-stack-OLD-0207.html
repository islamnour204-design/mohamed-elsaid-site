Layer 02  AdvisoryEnterprise and public sector

Sixteen years turning infrastructure decisions into commercial ones

At Intel I advise enterprise and public sector organisations on AI infrastructure, cloud architecture, and datacenter modernisation. Most of the work is unglamorous: sizing, migration economics, where a workload should actually run, and what happens to the operating bill once the pilot ends.

Before Intel, six years at Dell EMC, first as a level two support engineer on enterprise storage, then as a solutions architect. That period taught me how infrastructure fails in production, which is a different education from how it behaves on a slide.

Layer 04  ResearchThirteen papers, 2011 to 2025

A decade modelling what migration actually costs

Doctorate at the Hasso-Plattner Institute under Professor Christoph Meinel, applying machine learning to predicting the cost of virtual machine live migration. It began as an academic question about overhead. It turned out to describe the exact capacity problem enterprises now hit moving AI workloads into production.
